💡 What is QDS?

Unlike standard signatures, a Quantum Digital Signature (QDS) uses the laws of quantum mechanics to sign messages. Alice sends quantum states (using BB84 states) to Bob and Charlie. Because quantum states cannot be copied perfectly (No-Cloning Theorem) and measuring them changes them, Bob and Charlie can verify Alice's signature without anyone being able to forge it or deny it.
