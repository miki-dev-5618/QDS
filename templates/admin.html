The isolated test sends a baseline, attacks and probes R1 → R2, then sends fresh qubits through R3 while R1 → R2 remains quarantined. It uses the real simulator and does not change your active network.

Which attacks should cause rerouting?

Quantum edge fault: exclude that edge and choose another route. Classical Eve forgery: reject the forged revealed signature; this supplies no evidence that a quantum link failed. Signer or emergency stop: every route shares the signer, so choosing another relay cannot bypass that stop.

To test the live network: activate the multi-hop demo, send an authentic baseline, select R1 → R2 in the link controls, inject an intercept and probe that edge, then send again. Keep the failed edge quarantined. You should see the last and next routes change to R3 without a reset.
