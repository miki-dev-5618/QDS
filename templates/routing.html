Failover & rerouting

Failover self-test

Runs the full reroute scenario on a private copy of the multi-hop network: send via R1 → R2, attack and quarantine it, then confirm the next send goes via R3. Your live network is not touched.

How rerouting works

Each send chooses a route before preparing its qubits. Blocked quantum links are skipped; the failed link can stay quarantined while another route carries the next send.

Quantum edge fault: exclude that edge and choose another route. Classical Eve forgery: reject the forged revealed signature; this supplies no evidence that a quantum link failed. Signer or emergency stop: every route shares the signer, so choosing another relay cannot bypass that stop.

Live test: select the multi-hop demo network, send an authentic baseline from the console, select R1 → R2, inject an intercept and probe it, then send again. The next route should change to R3 without a reset.
